THE IDEA

Most physics is taught as symbols.
It deserves to be felt.

Equations are precise, but they hide the picture. A spherical harmonic is just a formula on paper — until you see it bloom into lobes and spin with its phase. This is a growing collection of tools that make the invisible structure of physics visible, hands-on, and intuitive.
